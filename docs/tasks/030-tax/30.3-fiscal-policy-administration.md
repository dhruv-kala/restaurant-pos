# Task 30.3 — Fiscal Policy Administration

## Status

Planned.

## Objective

Manage outlet-level fiscal rules and invoice sequencing.

## Scope

Implement:

- OutletFiscalPolicy
- FiscalInvoiceSequence

Do not implement:

- government e-invoice integration
- fiscal device integration
- accounting export

## Ownership

- fiscal year configuration
- invoice prefix configuration
- invoice numbering rules
- outlet-specific fiscal settings

## Invariants

- Invoice sequences are outlet scoped.
- Invoice numbers must be unique per outlet and fiscal year.
- Fiscal policies cannot cross tenant boundaries.
- Sequence changes must be audited.
- Generated invoice numbers must be append-only.

## Dependencies

Requires:

- Task 30.1
- Outlet Management
- Billing
- Receipt Module
- Audit

## Completion Criteria

- Outlet fiscal policy can be configured.
- Invoice sequences can be generated.
- Sequence uniqueness enforced.
- Audit events generated.
- Build passes.
