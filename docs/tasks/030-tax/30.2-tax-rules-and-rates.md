# Task 30.2 — Tax Rules and Rates

## Status

Planned.

## Objective

Provide configurable tax rates, groups, and category mappings.

## Scope

Implement:

- TaxRate
- TaxGroup
- TaxRule
- TaxCategoryMapping

Do not implement:

- fiscal invoice sequencing
- government integrations
- advanced reports
- UI

## Ownership

- tax rate configuration
- tax group composition
- item/category tax mapping
- rule activation windows

## Examples

- GST 5%
- GST 12%
- GST 18%
- CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%
- CGST 9% + SGST 9%

## Invariants

- Tax rates are immutable after use in finalized bills.
- Tax groups may contain multiple rates.
- Active date ranges must not conflict for the same mapping.
- Tenant isolation is enforced.

## Dependencies

Requires:

- Task 30.1
- Menu Management
- Billing
- Audit

## Completion Criteria

- Tax rates can be created.
- Tax groups can be created.
- Categories/items can be mapped to tax rules.
- Audit events generated.
- Build passes.
