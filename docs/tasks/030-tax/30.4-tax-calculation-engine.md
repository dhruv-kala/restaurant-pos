# Task 30.4 — Tax Calculation Engine

## Status

Planned.

## Objective

Implement deterministic tax calculation for orders, bills, receipts, and future customer ordering.

## Scope

Implement:

- tax calculation service
- inclusive/exclusive tax support
- tax breakdown generation
- tax calculation snapshot

Do not implement:

- tax filing
- government reporting
- accounting export

## Ownership

- tax calculation
- tax breakdown
- tax snapshots
- calculation explainability

## Inputs

- tenant
- outlet
- order/bill items
- item category
- tax profile
- tax group
- businessDate
- customer tax context if available

## Invariants

- Calculation must be deterministic.
- Finalized bills store snapshots.
- Historical bills are not recalculated from live rules.
- Tax calculation must return explainable breakdowns.
- Tenant isolation is enforced.

## Dependencies

Requires:

- Task 30.1
- Task 30.2
- Task 30.3
- Orders
- Billing
- Receipts

## Completion Criteria

- Tax calculation works.
- Inclusive and exclusive tax supported.
- Bill tax snapshots created.
- Build passes.
