# Task 30.5 — Tax Reporting Foundation

## Status

Planned.

## Objective

Provide tax reporting APIs for GST/VAT summaries and detailed tax breakdowns.

## Scope

Implement:

- tax summary report
- detailed tax report
- invoice count report
- taxable amount report

Do not implement:

- tax return filing
- government submission
- accounting export

## Ownership

- tax report aggregation
- businessDate-based reporting
- outlet tax summaries

## Reports

- Tax Summary
- GST Summary
- Taxable Sales
- Tax Collected
- Invoice Tax Detail

## Invariants

- Reports must use bill/receipt snapshots.
- Reports must use businessDate.
- Reports must not recalculate historical tax from current rules.
- Tenant isolation is enforced.

## Dependencies

Requires:

- Task 30.4
- Billing
- Receipts
- Reports Module

## Completion Criteria

- Tax summary works.
- Detailed tax report works.
- Outlet filtering works.
- Build passes.
