# Task 29.3 — Promotion Campaigns

## Status

Planned.

## Objective

Provide time-bound promotion campaigns for restaurants and outlets.

## Scope

Implement:

- PromotionCampaign
- PromotionRule
- campaign activation/deactivation
- outlet-scoped promotions

Do not implement:

- marketing automation
- customer segmentation
- communication sending
- AI promotion generation

## Ownership

- campaign configuration
- campaign rules
- campaign date windows
- outlet targeting

## Data Model

- PromotionCampaign
- PromotionRule

## Campaign Examples

- happy hour
- weekend offer
- festival discount
- new customer offer
- category promotion

## Invariants

- Campaigns are tenant scoped.
- Outlet campaigns cannot apply outside assigned outlets.
- Inactive campaigns cannot produce discounts.
- Expired campaigns cannot apply.
- Rule snapshots are preserved through discount applications.

## Dependencies

Requires:

- Task 29.1
- Task 29.2 optional
- Outlet Management
- RBAC
- Audit

## Completion Criteria

- Campaigns can be created.
- Campaigns can target outlets.
- Campaign rules can be evaluated.
- Audit events generated.
- Build passes.
