# Task 31.1 - Business Day Foundation

## Status

Complete.

## Objective

Create the business day model and outlet-level operational date management.

## Scope

Implemented:

- BusinessDay persistence model
- open business day
- close business day
- current business day lookup
- list business days

Deferred:

- shift sessions
- cash drawer sessions
- reconciliation
- business day close validations against unresolved operational exceptions
- administration UI

## Invariants

- One active business day per outlet.
- Business date is separate from system timestamp.
- Reports use `businessDate`.
- Business days are tenant and outlet scoped.
- Closed business days are immutable.
- Business days cannot be deleted.

## API

- `POST /business-days/open`
- `GET /business-days`
- `GET /business-days/current`
- `PATCH /business-days/:id/close`

## Authorization

Uses repository permission keys:

- `business_day.read`
- `business_day.open`
- `business_day.close`

Tenant administrators and managers can operate business days. Outlet-bound
users are limited to their assigned outlet unless they have tenant/platform
scope.

## Completion Criteria

- Business day lifecycle works.
- Outlet isolation enforced.
- Audit events generated for open and close.
