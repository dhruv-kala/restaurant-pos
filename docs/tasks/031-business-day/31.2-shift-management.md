# Task 31.2 - Shift Management

## Status

Complete.

## Objective

Provide operational shift lifecycle management.

## Scope

Implemented:

- ShiftSession persistence model
- open shift session
- close shift session
- current shift session lookup
- shift session listing
- optional link to existing staff shift templates

Deferred:

- cash drawer management
- shift reconciliation
- business day closing checks
- operations administration UI

## Invariants

- One active shift session per user.
- Shift session belongs to an outlet.
- Shift session belongs to an open business day.
- Shift sessions are tenant scoped.
- Closed shift sessions are immutable.
- Shift sessions cannot be deleted.

## API

- `POST /shift-sessions/open`
- `GET /shift-sessions`
- `GET /shift-sessions/current`
- `PATCH /shift-sessions/:id/close`

## Authorization

Uses repository permission keys:

- `shifts.read`
- `shifts.open`
- `shifts.close`

Tenant administrators and managers can operate outlet shift sessions. Cashiers
can open and close their own shift sessions when granted the operational shift
permissions.

## Completion Criteria

- Shift lifecycle works.
- Audit events generated.
