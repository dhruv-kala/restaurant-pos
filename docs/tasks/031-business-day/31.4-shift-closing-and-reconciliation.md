# Task 31.4 - Shift Closing and Reconciliation

Status: Complete

## Objective

Provide shift-end reconciliation.

## Scope

Implement:

* ShiftReconciliation

Features:

* expected cash
* counted cash
* variance
* approval notes

## Invariants

* Reconciliation occurs before shift closure.
* Variances are auditable.
* Historical reconciliations are immutable.

## Completion Criteria

* Reconciliation works.
* Variances tracked.

## Implementation Summary

Implemented:

* Tenant/outlet/business-day/shift-scoped `ShiftReconciliation`.
* One immutable reconciliation per shift session and cash drawer.
* Expected cash snapshot from the closed cash drawer.
* Counted cash and variance persistence.
* Required approval notes for non-zero variance.
* Reconciliation audit event with variance metadata.
* Shift close precondition requiring a recorded reconciliation.

Protected APIs:

* `POST /shift-reconciliations`
* `GET /shift-reconciliations`
* `GET /shift-reconciliations/:id`

Permissions:

* `shift_reconciliation.read`
* `shift_reconciliation.create`

Out of scope:

* Business day closing.
* Operations administration UI.
* Payment-to-drawer posting automation.
