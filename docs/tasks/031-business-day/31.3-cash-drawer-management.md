# Task 31.3 - Cash Drawer Management

## Status

Complete.

## Objective

Manage cashier cash drawers.

## Scope

Implemented:

- CashDrawer persistence model
- CashDrawerTransaction append-only history
- open drawer
- opening balance transaction
- cash in, cash out, and adjustment transactions
- current drawer lookup
- drawer transaction history
- close drawer with counted closing balance

Deferred:

- payment-to-cash-drawer posting
- shift reconciliation
- business day closing checks
- operations administration UI

## Invariants

- One active drawer per shift session.
- Transactions are append-only.
- Drawer belongs to tenant, outlet, business day, and shift session.
- Closed drawers are immutable.
- Drawers cannot be deleted.

## API

- `POST /cash-drawers/open`
- `GET /cash-drawers`
- `GET /cash-drawers/current`
- `GET /cash-drawers/:id/transactions`
- `POST /cash-drawers/:id/transactions`
- `PATCH /cash-drawers/:id/close`

## Authorization

Uses repository permission keys:

- `cash_drawer.read`
- `cash_drawer.open`
- `cash_drawer.adjust`
- `cash_drawer.close`

Tenant administrators and managers can operate outlet cash drawers. Cashiers can
operate drawers attached to their own shift sessions when granted operational
cash drawer permissions.

## Completion Criteria

- Cash drawer lifecycle works.
- Transaction history preserved.
