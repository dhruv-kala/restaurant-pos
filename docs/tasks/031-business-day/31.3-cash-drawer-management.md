# Task 31.3 — Cash Drawer Management

## Objective

Manage cashier cash drawers.

## Scope

Implement:

* CashDrawer
* CashDrawerTransaction

Features:

* open drawer
* opening balance
* cash adjustments
* close drawer

## Invariants

* One active drawer per shift.
* Transactions are append-only.
* Drawer belongs to outlet.

## Completion Criteria

* Cash drawer lifecycle works.
* Transaction history preserved.
