# Task 31.5 - Business Day Closing

Status: Complete

## Objective

Perform day-end operational closure.

## Scope

Implement:

- BusinessDayClosing

Features:

- close business day
- summary generation
- operational validation

## Invariants

- Active shifts must be closed first.
- Active drawers must be closed first.
- Shift sessions must be reconciled first.
- Closed business days become read-only.
- Business day closing summaries are immutable.

## Completion Criteria

- Day-end process works.
- Validation enforced.

## Implementation Summary

Implemented:

- Tenant/outlet/business-day-scoped `BusinessDayClosing`.
- One immutable closing summary per business day.
- Close-time operational validation for active shifts, active drawers, and
  unreconciled shifts.
- Summary snapshot with shift count, drawer count, reconciliation count,
  expected cash, counted cash, variance, currency, actor, notes, and close time.
- Business day close transaction that validates, performs optimistic version
  close, creates the closing summary, and records audit events atomically.
- Read endpoint for the immutable closing summary.

Protected APIs:

- `PATCH /business-days/:id/close`
- `GET /business-days/:id/closing`

Out of scope:

- Operations administration UI.
- Accounting export.
- Payment-to-drawer posting automation.
