# Task 39.1 - Fulfillment Method and Policy Foundation

## Status

Planned.

## Objective

Define tenant and outlet fulfillment methods, policies, service hours, minimum
order rules, and fee configuration.

## Scope

Implement:

* fulfillment method enums
* `FulfillmentPolicy` schema
* tenant default and outlet override precedence
* service hours and minimum order validation
* policy read/evaluation service
* protected policy APIs

## Deferred

* delivery zones
* fulfillment order lifecycle
* capacity slots
* UI

## Completion Criteria

* Outlet override behavior is explicit.
* Disabled fulfillment methods are rejected by evaluation.
* Policies are tenant isolated.
