# Task 39.2 - Customer Address and Zone Management

## Status

Planned.

## Objective

Add customer delivery addresses and outlet delivery zones used to determine
delivery eligibility and fees.

## Scope

Implement:

* `DeliveryZone` schema
* `CustomerDeliveryAddress` schema or customer-address extension
* zone eligibility evaluation service
* fee and minimum-order rules
* protected zone APIs
* customer address APIs

## Deferred

* map routing
* geocoding provider integration
* driver assignment
* UI

## Completion Criteria

* Zones are tenant and outlet scoped.
* Customer addresses are not visible across tenants.
* Eligibility results include explicit denial reasons.
